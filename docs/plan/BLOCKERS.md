## Backlog rules

- Rows are append-only evidence records. Resolution narrows or supersedes a row with receipts;
  it does not delete the original obligation or dissent.
- `resolved` requires the stated exit condition and an independent courtroom disposition.
- `deferred (review by YYYY-MM-DD)` requires a recorded court decision and a new review date.
- A missed review date keeps the blocker `open`; it does not waive or silently resolve it.
- Later phases append newly discovered blockers here and may update only the status and
  evidence pointers of obligations they actually resolve.
