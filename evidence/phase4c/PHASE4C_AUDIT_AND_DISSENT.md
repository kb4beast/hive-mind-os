## Preserved dissent

- Visible development fixtures are not holdouts and can become contaminated by
  training or implementation exposure.
- Reference and digest validation proves measurement integrity, not that referenced
  artifacts are semantically correct.
- Generation Zero may accept development observations; Explorer v2 cannot. This
  means no comparison or candidate-behavior conclusion is possible.
- Integer scores can create false precision. The metric vector and safety floors
  must remain visible, and no aggregate may authorize promotion.
- Static stopping and loop categories are vocabulary until a separately governed
  runtime emits executable receipts.
- No customer value, learning, champion, activation, promotion, or superiority
  claim is admitted.
