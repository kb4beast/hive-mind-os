## Method note

Market sizing in the Boom Baseline deck is deliberately presented as a **ladder of
graded figures with the assumption stated**, rather than as a single TAM number. That is
a choice: a fabricated TAM is the fastest way to lose a technical investor, and the
honest position, that the analogous market is $343M a year while spaceport spend is
zero, is stronger than an invented multiplication. O15 is what converts that ladder
into a real bottom-up number.
