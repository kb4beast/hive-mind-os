# Coupon Hive Terms

- Effective date: September 14, 2026
Version: 0.1.0 draft

## Service

Coupon Hive provides informational research about publicly available promotions, prices, and free offers. It may prepare a recurring task request for a supported OpenAI host. It does not sell products, process payments, complete checkout, reserve inventory, enroll users, enter giveaways, or guarantee an offer.

## Offer accuracy

Offers can change or disappear without notice. “Verified” means a cited source was observed at the stated time; it does not guarantee inventory, geographic eligibility, final tax or shipping, code acceptance, or future availability. Users should review the merchant's current terms before acting.

Coupon Hive distinguishes unconditional free offers from purchase-required, rebate, trial, subscription, BOGO, sample, and sweepstakes offers. Unknown terms remain unknown rather than being treated as favorable.

## Acceptable use

Use Coupon Hive only for lawful, publicly authorized offers. Do not use it to:

- guess or brute-force codes;
- exploit access controls or obvious pricing errors;
- use leaked, employee-only, stolen, or single-use codes without authorization;
- farm accounts, referrals, trials, or new-customer eligibility;
- impersonate another person or misstate eligibility;
- seek counterfeit, stolen, pirated, fraudulent, prohibited, or regulated goods or services contrary to applicable law or OpenAI policy;
- submit credentials, payment details, identity documents, or other sensitive data.

The skill may refuse or quarantine unsafe, deceptive, unauthorized, or unsupported requests.

## External sources

Merchant, organizer, government, and third-party sites control their own content, availability, terms, privacy practices, transactions, and any attribution they add. Coupon Hive does not endorse a source merely by linking to it. The publisher adds no affiliate links, receives no referral compensation in version 0.1.0, never ranks by commission, and removes common tracking parameters when presenting canonical links. It cannot guarantee that a source or host result contains no third-party attribution.

## Scheduled tasks

A watch exists only when the OpenAI host returns a successful recurrence receipt: a Scheduled Task in ChatGPT or an automation in Codex. Creation or update requires confirmation of the exact title, prompt, scope, schedule, and requested actions. Standalone pause, resume, and delete actions require fresh confirmation for the exact task. The user controls each recurrence in the host product. Removing Coupon Hive may not delete an existing recurrence. A version mismatch, missing deterministic validator, or missing search capability should cause a mutation or run to fail closed.

## Changes and support

Material changes require a new version and release notes. See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for issue reporting. These draft terms must be reviewed and made available at a stable public URL before Store submission.
