# Phase 5C Dissent

1. **Procedural review is correlated.** One assistant performed separate role passes. The
   resulting attack coverage is useful, but it is not authenticated independent review and
   must not be labeled as such.
2. **A strict planning contract can become bureaucratic.** The requirement/change/test/evidence/
   rollback/artifact closure rules may reject legitimate future small changes. Any relaxation
   requires a new ADR and adversarial regressions; it cannot be weakened merely to make a
   request pass.
3. **Planning is not execution.** The names `Builder`, `test_plan`, and
   `execution_evidence_plan` may create a false impression that work occurred. Every output
   therefore fixes observed-effect and authority claims false, but UI and future host
   projections must preserve that distinction.
4. **Known dependency admission is conservative.** Unknown or quarantined dependencies are
   rejected instead of represented as executable options. A future source-resolution workflow
   may produce a new admitted request; Phase 5C must not silently normalize uncertainty.
5. **Resource values are proposed accounting.** They are not a lease and do not enforce cost,
   token, time, or tool ceilings. Phase 6 must implement hierarchical leases and reserves.
6. **Leaf resealing coverage is structural, not behavioral quality.** Rejecting 768 mutations
   proves canonical binding for the example, not that the Builder produces useful plans for
   real repositories.
7. **Merge and activation remain deferred.** Green CI is necessary but does not close B-OPS-09,
   establish P14-P20, satisfy source/license appeals, or prove release readiness.
