## Short description

Find current deals and genuine freebies, then build quiet watches for meaningful changes.

## Long description

Coupon Hive starts with a short, adaptive interview so “good deal” and “free” mean what you intend. It researches physical consumer goods, price drops, public coupon codes, manufacturer samples, and genuinely free public or community activities and events.

It prefers official sources, timestamps every verification, exposes shipping, fees, memberships, rebates, trials, renewals, eligibility, geography, stock, and expiry, and de-duplicates the same offer across multiple pages. Verified matches are kept separate from unverified leads.

When you want ongoing help, Coupon Hive prepares a low-noise watch and, only on a supported and entitled surface, asks the host to create it after you review and confirm the exact title, prompt, scope, schedule, and requested actions. ChatGPT uses Scheduled Tasks; Codex uses separate automations. It requests alerts only for a new verified match, a threshold-crossing improvement, a material term correction, or an expiry warning you requested. A watch is never claimed until the host returns a receipt; account limits, unavailable recurrence, or an unsafe payload rewrite can prevent creation.

Coupon Hive never purchases, signs up, enters a giveaway, tests a code at checkout, or asks for payment credentials. Version 0.1.0 has no publisher backend, publisher-added affiliate links, referral compensation, advertising, or user tracking.

The Store release does not recommend digital products, paid services, subscription plans or upgrades, travel booking, regulated commerce, or prohibited goods. It can flag a trial or subscription only to prevent a misleading free claim.

## Starter prompts

1. Interview me and find the best currently verifiable deal for something I want.
2. Help me define what free means, then find genuine freebies near me or online.
3. Turn my shopping request into a quiet recurring watch that only reports meaningful changes.
