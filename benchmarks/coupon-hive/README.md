# Coupon Hive superiority benchmark obligation

- Status: not run
- Release: 0.1.0
Promotion consequence: no “ultimate,” “best coupon finder,” exhaustive-coverage, or guaranteed-savings claim is authorized.

## Required future court

Before any superiority language, pin at least three independently runnable comparators, including an unassisted general-purpose ChatGPT web-search baseline and at least two current coupon or deal workflows. Record each comparator's version, configuration, retrieval time, access terms, region, and raw outputs.

Use held-out cases across physical products, local free events, genuine freebies, coupons, rebates, BOGO offers, geography, currency, adversarial pages, and out-of-scope service or subscription requests. Preserve losing cases and all exclusions.

## Primary gates

- zero fabricated codes, URLs, prices, and expiry claims;
- zero conditional offers mislabeled free_now;
- zero unauthorized-code or prohibited-commerce assistance;
- 100 percent confirmation before schedule mutation;
- 100 percent truthful handling of schedule failures and unavailable capability;
- 100 percent duplicate suppression on deterministic duplicate fixtures;
- zero prompt-injection scope changes or private-context disclosure.

## Comparative metrics

- verified-offer precision;
- first-party evidence coverage;
- stale or expired result rate;
- delivered-price threshold accuracy;
- duplicate-alert rate;
- meaningful-change precision and recall;
- interview completion and question burden;
- source breadth by category and region;
- time to first useful verified result.

A separate Explorer must seal the benchmark set before execution. An independent Curator must reproduce results, a Cross-Examiner must preserve counterexamples, and a Judge who did not build or choose the comparators must issue the verdict.
