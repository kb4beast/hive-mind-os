# EXF-010 — Scouted candidate field

**Node:** `EXF-010` · **Round:** R2 (parallel with EXF-020) · **Roles:** Explorer, Architect
**Stopping condition:** additional scouting produces duplicates or dominated variants rather than materially new mechanisms, and that saturation is recorded.

Scouting was scoped by the subject truth: candidates must be enterable into an Expanding
Frontiers program by a South Texas applicant, and must plausibly reach a cash prize or
grant. Twenty-seven candidates across five mechanism families survived a first pass;
filler was not manufactured to reach a count.

## Saturation record

Scouting was continued until new searching returned materially the same mechanisms.
Saturation was declared on three signals:

1. **Mechanism repetition.** New candidates collapsed into families already represented —
   further "space × energy" ideas reduced to variants of C1/C2, further NASA-transfer
   ideas to variants of B1–B4.
2. **Dominance.** Late candidates were dominated on the rubric by an existing entrant in
   the same family (e.g. C5 waste-heat recovery is dominated by C1 on every criterion).
3. **Off-thesis drift.** Broader searching produced generic space startups with no South
   Texas anchor, which the regional-impact veto eliminates before scoring.

Twenty-seven candidates is a deliberate stopping point, not a target: the families are
exhaustive of the mechanisms ExF's programs can actually route a company through
(license NASA IP, join space to energy, build regional workforce, sell software/data).
