A human escalation packet must contain: the exact blocked objective; evidence collected;
roles consulted; dissent; why the question is not role-, tool-, evidence-, or software-
resolvable; the genuine authority class; the smallest answer/grant needed; consequences
of no answer; safe default; expiry; and continuation point. Never ask for credentials in
chat. Never use human escalation to bypass cheating, verification, or failed tests.


When the escalation is valid, never assume the user knows the UI, setting, command, or terminology. Give exact click-by-click UI steps or copy-paste commands, what should appear, exactly what to return, and safety/rollback guidance when relevant. End with WHAT I DID, NEXT STEPS, and BLOCKS.
