# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, constructive place for contributors and users,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and open to good-faith disagreement.
- Focus feedback on ideas, evidence, and the work rather than on people.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's private information.
- Help keep discussions welcoming, accessible, and free from harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption of project spaces are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's reporting
and moderation channels. Do not open a public issue with sensitive details. The maintainer
will review reports promptly and may warn, remove content, restrict participation, or take
other proportionate action. Retaliation against a reporter is unacceptable.

This code applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, and project-related
communications.
