# Phase 4B dissent

- A deterministic definition proves composition plumbing, not rich Explorer
  behavior, curiosity, usefulness, or customer value.
- Python-held canonical data preserves the judged installed-resource count but
  creates portability debt for a later cross-language resource migration.
- The successor inherits requested capabilities as metadata while granting none;
  it cannot perform repository, history, web, or analysis work.
- A typed playbook can still become ornamental prose until held-out behavioral
  episodes prove that its lenses, counterarguments, bridges, and stopping rules
  change outcomes.
- Exact and structured opportunity convergence exists, but semantic escape and
  false-merge rates remain unmeasured.
- No champion pointer, activation, promotion, learning, or superiority claim is
  admitted.
