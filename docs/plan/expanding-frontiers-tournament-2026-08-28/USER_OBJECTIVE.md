# User Objective and Session Directives

## Original subject supplied

> Use hive-mind-os on a new branch from latest main to find me top 3 ideas to
> compete and put into https://www.expandingfrontiers.org
>
> Just use the subject dag to come up with best winner on idea to get a grant or award.

## SUBJECT as interpreted

> Identify the venture idea most likely to WIN a grant or award from Expanding
> Frontiers (ExF), the Brownsville, Texas 501(c)(3) space-and-energy innovation
> ecosystem builder, and produce the top three entries plus an executable plan
> for the winner.

## Governing interpretation

1. **The objective is winning, not being interesting.** The scoring system is
   derived from what ExF is actually rewarded for as a nonprofit ecosystem
   builder (regional job creation, NASA technology commercialization, the
   space-energy crossover thesis), not from generic venture-capital criteria.
   An idea that a Sand Hill Road partner would fund and an ExF judging panel
   would pass over is a losing entry here.
2. **Non-dilutive prize scale sets the physics.** The flagship ExF Space Tech
   Pitch Competition awards a reported $32,000 total across the best three
   business plans. Capital efficiency is therefore a first-class criterion: an
   idea whose next honest milestone costs $2M cannot be de-risked by this prize
   and scores badly no matter how good the technology is.
3. **Geography is a scoring dimension, not background.** ExF's own funding
   (EDA STEM Challenge, DOE EPIC Prize, SBA Growth Accelerator Fund) is
   justified on Rio Grande Valley economic development. An idea that could be
   executed from anywhere is worth materially less to them than one that must
   be executed within 30 km of Brownsville.
4. **Evidence discipline applies (AGENTS.md courtroom rule).** Several primary
   sources were unreachable from this environment. Nothing from a blocked
   source is invented. Each such item is recorded as a blocking evidence
   obligation in `SOURCE_REGISTER.md` and carried into the execution DAG as a
   node the founder must discharge before submitting.
5. **The founder's existing assets are part of the field.** This repository is
   a real, working, differentiated asset owned by the entrant. Ignoring it
   would have been an analytical error; overstating its maturity would have
   been a dishonest one. It is entered as a competitor and judged like every
   other entrant, including on its README's own admission of prototype status.

## Method

The repository's own `prompts/shared/ULTIMATE_SOLUTION_TOURNAMENT.md`
(GenericPrompt) was executed against the SUBJECT: dynamic criteria construction
(section 4), a 30-entrant field (section 6), triple-elimination scoring
(section 7), adversarial attack on leaders (section 8), component-level winners
(section 10), hybrid challengers (section 11), and a final championship
(section 13), followed by the Part II conversion of the winner into a
machine-readable execution DAG with node contracts (section 17).
