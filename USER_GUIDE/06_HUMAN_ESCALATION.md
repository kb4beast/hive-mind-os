# Human Escalation Boundary

The system is designed for no avoidable human interaction, not fabricated authority.

A valid escalation packet must show:

- the exact blocked action;
- the genuine authority class;
- evidence already gathered;
- roles consulted and their dissent;
- why role/tool/repair/replan routes are exhausted;
- the minimum decision or credential required;
- safe alternatives;
- consequences of no response; and
- rollback/expiry of any granted authority.

Human escalation is valid only for credentials/secrets, legal or regulatory signoff, financial
spend, production access, protected-branch merge, owner value choice, personal consent, or an
external contractual commitment. The controller rejects `software_bug`, `ambiguity`, `missing
evidence`, or `suspected_cheating` presented as human authority.
