# Receipt-capture design v1

This is the forward-looking, design-only court authorized by
`CASE-VALIDATION-PROVENANCE-REMAND-QUALIFICATION`.  It designs the durable
receipt contract needed to prevent a recurrence of missing validation
provenance.  It neither implements nor evaluates that contract.

The five nodes are deliberately ordered: the Clerk seals this contract;
independent Architect and Curator seats produce the design and adversarial
review in parallel; an Integrator reconciles them; and a different Judge may
authorize **only a separately sealed native implementation-proposal DAG**.
The Judge may grant that limited opening only when the design has zero
unresolved material findings.  It cannot authorize implementation directly.

The proposed design must specify an ordered discovery vector; exactly one
terminal outcome per ID, including lifecycle and class skips; a declared and
hashed label vocabulary; raw-stream hashes and an explicit parser grammar;
summary reconciliation; source/tree/interpreter/environment binding;
privacy-sensitive redaction; atomic receipt write and retention; and failure
and rollback behavior.  The rejected fixture candidate `41950b74bdec2b6e1c48ee7f5ef3ce947d0c8378`
and non-promoted GCO candidate `d02c2d2` are retained adverse evidence and
may not be reused, relabeled, qualified, or composed.
