# Phase 5B dissent

The Architect candidate is structurally strong but deliberately incomplete.

1. It evaluates caller-supplied options; it does not prove that the option set is
   complete or that the repository was independently inspected.
2. Integer-ppm scoring is deterministic, not evidence that its weights predict
   customer value or design quality.
3. The candidate can identify a provisional viable option but cannot select it.
4. Caller-supplied evidence remains a reference, not authenticated verification.
5. Procedural role labels are not independent actors.
6. Resource allocations are a plan, not physical leases or observed consumption.
7. Verification records prove coverage structure, not that tests or experiments ran.
8. The candidate has no durable mission storage, provider/tool binding, scheduler,
   or active runtime.
9. The 442-leaf reseal suite is broad adversarial evidence, not a proof that no other
   semantic attack exists.
10. No customer value, learning, promotion, production readiness, release readiness,
    or superiority claim is admitted.

A future challenger must preserve these dissent points until separately resolved with
receipts. Passing CI does not erase them.
